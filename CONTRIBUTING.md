# Contributing to HopBound

Thank you for your interest. At this stage the most valuable contributions are:

1. **Challenges to the method.** If a principle in the README or a definition
   in `spec/` is wrong or incomplete, open an issue and say why.
2. **Representative pipelines.** Nominate a perception or control path from
   real use that a campaign should measure. The choice of what to measure
   shapes everything downstream and should not be made by one person.
3. **Measurements from other platforms**, once the reporting format is
   published.

## Ground rules

- **Sign off every commit** (`git commit -s`). The `Signed-off-by` line
  certifies the [Developer Certificate of Origin](https://developercertificate.org/):
  you wrote the contribution or have the right to submit it under this
  project's licences.
- **Bring only what you may share.** No employer-confidential or
  customer-confidential material, and nothing copied from projects whose
  licence or ownership does not allow it.
- **Label your evidence.** Say whether a statement is measured, derived,
  or an expectation. A hypothesis that failed, with its attribution, is a
  useful result.
- **Integrate, don't duplicate.** Where an existing tool already does a job
  well (tracing frameworks, chain-analysis tools), HopBound should use it
  rather than reimplement it.
